# Partner Reliability Index

## Purpose

The index supports prioritization and monitoring. It is not a public accusation, legal finding, credit rating, or guarantee. Results must be explainable, evidence-linked, reviewable, and access-controlled.

## Evidence Categories

- Identity and beneficial ownership verified
- Business licence and tax/customs registration
- Import/export history and source lineage
- Previous contracts and completed shipments
- Payment punctuality, where lawfully evidenced
- Quality acceptance and claims history
- Document accuracy
- Shipment performance
- Responsiveness and communication
- Trade references with consent
- Inspection/provider history
- Disputes, defaults, sanctions, or adverse findings

## Suggested Scoring

Use separate dimensions rather than one number:

- Identity confidence: 0-100
- Trade-history confidence: 0-100
- Performance reliability: 0-100
- Quality/document reliability: 0-100
- Payment reliability: 0-100
- Current risk status: Green, Amber, Red, or Unknown

Do not score a partner on missing data as if it were bad performance. Use “Unknown” and request evidence.

## Governance

Every material entry records source, date, evidence grade, analyst, consent basis where applicable, confidence, expiry/review date, and challenge process. A partner may correct or challenge an entry. Confidential references remain internal and are not disclosed to another party without authority.

Past successful business is positive evidence, not automatic approval. One strong reference cannot override sanctions, identity, document, or current credit concerns.
