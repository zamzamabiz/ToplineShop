# Standards and Source Register

## How to Use This Register

The system must distinguish binding law, incorporated contract rules, technical standards, service-provider evidence, market intelligence, and internal TLS policy. A source may support a decision without automatically creating a legal obligation.

## Source Evidence Grades

- **A:** official government, regulator, association, bank, accredited laboratory, or directly verifiable original record
- **B:** named inspection provider, licensed commercial data provider, recognized registry, or signed trade document
- **C:** documented trade reference or consent-based partner statement with identifiable source
- **D:** public secondary report or unverified market intelligence
- **E:** hearsay, anonymous claim, or unsupported assertion

Grade D and E information may create a research lead but cannot alone approve a partner, contract, payment term, or quality claim.

## Research Boundaries

Public availability does not automatically prove accuracy, unrestricted commercial reuse, or personal-data permission. Each dataset must record source terms, access method, date, field coverage, license or use basis, known limitations, and whether the data is suitable for publication, internal screening, or CRM use only.

GAFTA and FOSFA forms may be copyrighted or access-controlled. ERP64 should build structured operational fields and licensed references, not copy protected contract text into an unlicensed platform.
