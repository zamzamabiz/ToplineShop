# Commodity Marketplace Implementation Roadmap

## Gate M0 - Research and Policy Freeze — APPROVED 2026-09-13

The Business Owner approved the M0 baseline and the recorded decisions in [M0-DECISION-RECORD.md](M0-DECISION-RECORD.md).

M0 approval authorizes preparation of M1 #FDD materials. It does not authorize deep backend, live payment, real partner-data use, or production trade execution.

## Gate M1 - Business Workflow and #FDD

Visually prove invite-only onboarding, masked profiles, sell offer, buy bid, match explanation, agent queue, consent, quotation, contract status, inspection, documents, shipment, payment status, claims, and monitoring using fixtures. No real partner data and no millions of rows in the browser.

Exit: visual evidence and owner-approved frontend freeze.

## Gate M2 - Controlled Pilot

Implement one corridor and a small number of commodities. Support offers, bids, matching, leads, internal messaging, partner evidence, approvals, contract pack tracking, and shipment milestones.

Exit: repeated pilot deals or simulated full workflows with evidence and no unresolved critical stop.

## Gate M3 - Trade Operations

Add inspection-provider directory, document comparison, quality/sample chain of custody, bank/payment status, claims, agent metrics, and partner reliability history.

Exit: controlled end-to-end operating procedure and audit results.

## Gate M4 - Broader Origin and Product Coverage

Add more Canada, USA, Australia, India, Pakistan, and other origin/destination profiles; then expand to oils, spices, feed, and additional commodities through commodity annexes.

Exit: country-specific compliance packs, provider coverage, support process, and measured risk limits.

## Gate M5 - Open Marketplace

Consider public registration, broader discovery, subscriptions, API access, paid verification, logistics integrations, and selected payment partners only after the invite-only model demonstrates reliable operation.

## Gate M6 - Commercial Scale

Review legal structure, licensing, data rights, insurance, credit products, custody of funds, privacy, support, recovery, security, and independent deployment. These are separate approvals, not automatic consequences of marketplace growth.

## Work Limits

One active marketplace frontend stream, one shared-platform stream, and one research or compliance stream. New commodities and countries enter through a controlled change request, not informal feature growth.
