# Payment, Risk, and Credit Controls

## Pilot Boundary

TLS does not finance, guarantee, insure, custody, release, or settle trade funds. Buyer and seller use their banks. TLS records agreed terms, document milestones, payment status supplied by the parties, and its own commission.

## Payment Ladder

1. Advance payment: lowest seller exposure, highest buyer exposure.
2. Sight LC: bank documentary process, subject to bank and document compliance.
3. CAD/D/P: documents against payment; suitable only under agreed bank instructions.
4. D/A or deferred payment: seller carries post-shipment credit exposure; restricted.
5. Open account: permitted only after explicit approval, credit limit, and risk mitigation.

Documentary collections do not provide the same payment guarantee as a letter of credit; banks facilitate document exchange and may not guarantee payment or document truth. [U.S. Trade Finance guidance](https://www.trade.gov/documentary-collections)

## Deal Risk Calculator

Use a transparent, explainable model rather than a single opaque score. Score each category 0-5, where 0 is low risk and 5 is severe risk:

| Category | Suggested weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Counterparty identity and authority | 15% |
| Previous trade performance | 15% |
| Credit and payment capacity | 20% |
| Country, bank, sanctions, and political risk | 15% |
| Commodity and quality risk | 10% |
| Document and fraud risk | 10% |
| Logistics and route risk | 10% |
| TLS concentration and reputation exposure | 5% |

Weighted score = sum of category score multiplied by its weight. Any mandatory stop condition overrides the total score.

Suggested states:

- 0.0-1.0: low; routine controls
- 1.1-2.0: moderate; manager review
- 2.1-3.0: high; senior and control-owner approval
- 3.1-5.0: critical; stop unless owner-approved exception with mitigation

## Deferred-Term Approval

The seller must explicitly approve the buyer, tenor, amount, currency, credit limit, security, insurance, and default procedure for each deferred deal. The system must prevent an agent from converting CAD/D/P into D/A or open account without a new approval event.

## Stop Conditions

Identity uncertainty, sanctions concern, forged or inconsistent documents, unsupported quality claim, unverifiable bank instruction, suspicious urgency, unacceptable country or route risk, missing import permission, expired offer, undisclosed conflict, credit limit breach, or TLS exposure beyond policy.

Trade credit insurance, factoring, guarantees, or export-credit support may reduce receivables risk, but must be arranged by qualified providers and confirmed before credit is offered. [UK export non-payment guidance](https://www.business.gov.uk/export-from-uk/learn/categories/funding-financing-and-getting-paid/get-paid/insure-against-non-payment/)
