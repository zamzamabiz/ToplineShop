# Roles, RACI, and Agent SOP

## Roles

- **Platform Owner:** policy, risk appetite, partner categories, exceptions, disputes, and release approval.
- **Trade Manager:** commercial strategy, pricing, margin, allocation, and deal approval.
- **TLS Trade Agent:** qualifies matches, facilitates communication, records consent, drives next actions, and escalates.
- **Broker:** introduces a party or opportunity under a written mandate and commission agreement.
- **Seller:** warrants authority, availability, description, and contract performance.
- **Buyer:** warrants authority, demand, import capability, and payment capacity.
- **Compliance Officer:** KYB/KYC, beneficial ownership, sanctions, country, source, and reputational review.
- **Quality Coordinator:** inspection appointment, sampling, laboratory, certificate, and variance review.
- **Logistics Coordinator:** booking, ports, containers/vessels, notices, bill of lading, and milestones.
- **Finance Officer:** bank instructions, invoice, payment status, commission, exposure, and reconciliation.
- **Legal/Standards Reviewer:** contract family, clauses, licenses, disputes, and jurisdictional escalation.

## RACI Summary

| Activity | Responsible | Accountable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish offer/bid | Seller/Buyer | Trade Agent |
| Match screening | System | Trade Manager |
| Identity/compliance | Compliance | Platform Owner |
| Commercial negotiation | Agent/Trader | Trade Manager |
| Quality/inspection | Quality Coordinator | Trade Manager |
| Contract execution | Buyer/Seller | Legal/Standards Reviewer |
| Bank/payment status | Parties’ banks/Finance | Finance Officer |
| Shipment execution | Logistics Coordinator | Trade Manager |
| Stop/reject decision | Relevant control owner | Platform Owner for exceptions |
| Closure and lessons | Trade Agent | Trade Manager |

## Agent Procedure

1. Accept only a system-generated or approved manual lead.
2. Check offer/bid validity and hard-rule screening.
3. Confirm identity, authority, genuine demand, availability, and no conflict.
4. Send a controlled summary without exposing unnecessary contacts.
5. Obtain written interest and consent to proceed.
6. Collect missing commercial, quality, import, and payment information.
7. Route risk issues to compliance, quality, finance, or legal.
8. Record the negotiated term sheet; never rely on informal chat alone.
9. Obtain buyer and seller approval before quotation or contract issue.
10. Track contract, inspection, shipment, documents, payment, claims, and closure.

Agents may not alter system records, approve their own commission, waive mandatory checks, promise payment, certify quality, or disclose protected contacts without authorization.
