# Quality, Inspection, and Document SOP

## Control Principle

Quality is a contract claim supported by a defined method, sample, time, place, provider, and certificate. An SGS, Cotecna, government, or laboratory report is evidence within its stated scope; it is not a universal guarantee.

## Inspection Appointment

Before loading or sampling, record commodity, lot, contract, location, timing, quantity, sampling plan, tests, provider, provider accreditation or approved-register status, cost, report recipient, and whether the result is final at loading or discharge.

## Sample Chain of Custody

Record sample ID, lot, seal, increment locations, date/time, sampler, witnesses, split samples, storage, transfer, laboratory, method, result, report version, and retained-sample period. A result without traceable sample identity is incomplete.

## Minimum Document Review

- Commercial invoice
- Packing list or weight list
- Bill of lading draft and final copy
- Certificate of origin
- Phytosanitary certificate where required
- Fumigation certificate where required
- Quality and weight certificate
- Inspection report
- Insurance certificate where applicable
- Import permits and destination approvals
- Contract, amendments, and bank presentation record

The system compares document fields against the deal record and flags name, quantity, grade, origin, container, seal, port, dates, and price inconsistencies.

## Variance Handling

Pass, minor variance, contract variance, regulatory failure, document failure, suspected fraud, reinspection requested, claim opened, and stop shipment. Only an authorized party may waive a contractual or regulatory requirement.

## Provider Rules

Maintain a provider directory with office, method, accreditation, GAFTA/FOSFA register status where relevant, commodities, countries, insurance, report verification method, conflicts, and performance history. Provider selection must be neutral, contract-compatible, and approved by the parties.
