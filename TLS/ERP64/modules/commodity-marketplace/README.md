## Governing Boundaries

- TLS is a facilitator, broker, and managed marketplace operator; buyer and seller remain the contracting parties.
- TLS does not initially finance, guarantee, custody, insure, or settle trade funds.
- Initial access is invitation-only. Public registration and broader discovery are later gates.
- Automated matching creates leads; it never creates an offer, acceptance, credit approval, contract, or guarantee.
- Templates are operational data forms and checklists, not legal substitutes for licensed contract forms or professional advice.
- #FDD and #123 remain mandatory.
