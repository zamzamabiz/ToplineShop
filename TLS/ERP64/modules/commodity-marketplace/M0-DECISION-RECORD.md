# Gate M0 Decision Record

Decision date: 2026-09-13
Decision authority: Business Owner
Decision: APPROVED

## Approved Baseline

- Pakistan-focused, invite-only managed marketplace pilot.
- Later expansion to an open marketplace after verification, operations, privacy, and risk gates are proven.
- Initial commodities: rice, wheat, lentils, chickpeas, peas, corn, soybean meal, and selected oilseeds.
- Initial research and supply corridors: Pakistan, Canada, USA, Australia, India, and selected additional origins.
- TLS acts as facilitator, broker, managed marketplace operator, and agent-coordination platform.
- Buyer and seller remain the contracting parties.
- TLS earns an agreed brokerage, facilitation, or success fee.
- Payments initially remain directly between the parties and their banks.
- TLS does not initially finance, guarantee, insure, custody, release, or settle trade funds.
- Previous verified trade relationships are preferred but do not replace current checks.
- Public and licensed sources, official customs/trade data, inspection evidence, trade references, and consent-based intelligence may support screening, with evidence grades and source lineage.
- Every offer, bid, match, credit term, inspection result, contract, and deferred-payment arrangement requires explicit relevant-party and TLS authorization.
- TLS may pause, reject, or stop a deal for identity, compliance, quality, document, payment, country, logistics, legal, reputational, or data concerns.
- Standards are applied by relevance: applicable law, GAFTA/FOSFA contract reference, ICC rules, Codex/destination requirements, qualified inspection and laboratory evidence, and TLS workflow controls.
- #FDD and #123 remain mandatory.

## M1 Authorization

M0 approval authorizes the preparation of the M1 frontend workflow package using realistic mock or fixture data. M1 must visually prove invitation, registration, verification, masked profiles, sell offers, buy bids, matching, agent queue, consent, quotation, contract status, inspection, documents, shipment, payment status, claims, and monitoring.

M1 does not authorize deep backend, live payment integration, real partner data, or production trading until frontend freeze and the next approved gate.
