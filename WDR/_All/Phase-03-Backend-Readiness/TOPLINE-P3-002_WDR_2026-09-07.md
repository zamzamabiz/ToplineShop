## BACKEND STACK DECISION SUMMARY

**DECISION: Option C — Hybrid (Next.js Frontend + NestJS Backend)**

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
|--------|------|------|---------|
| A: Next.js Full-Stack | Single codebase, simple | Tight coupling, weak for multi-vendor | Rejected |
| B: Express/Fastify | Lightweight | Less opinionated, manual patterns | Rejected |
| **C: Hybrid NestJS** | **Enterprise patterns, DI, modular, independent scaling** | **Two services** | **SELECTED** |

**Rationale:** Multi-vendor SaaS + B2B + payment safety + future AI requires clean separation.
