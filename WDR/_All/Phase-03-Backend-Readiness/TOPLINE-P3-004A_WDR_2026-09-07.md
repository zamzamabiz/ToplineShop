## SAAS-FIRST POLICY SUMMARY

- Subscription-ready architecture
- Tenant-based access
- Configurable modules per tenant
- No single-business hardcoding
- Scalable for multiple organizations

## MULTI-TENANT POLICY SUMMARY

- Every business record belongs to a tenant where applicable
- Tenant isolation mandatory
- Cross-tenant access forbidden unless explicit policy allows
- Tenant-aware audit logs, feature flags, billing, module controls

## MULTI-BUSINESS / MULTI-VERTICAL SUMMARY

Future modules for: E-commerce, Wholesale, FMCG, Consulting, Property/Broker, Doctors/Appointments, Hospital/Clinic, Schools/Education, Service Business.

## PLATFORM CORE VS BUSINESS MODULES SUMMARY

Platform Core: Tenant, Auth/RBAC, Users, Module Registry, Audit, Notifications, Owner/Admin, Financial Safety, API Standards, Data Isolation.

Business Modules: Marketplace (active), B2B Wholesale (active), CRM, ERP, FMCG, Consulting, Property, Doctors, Hospital, School, Service.

## CRM FUTURE POLICY SUMMARY

Introduced earlier as marketplace relationship management. Scope: customer/vendor/buyer CRM, leads, inquiries, support, pipeline tracking.

## ERP FUTURE POLICY SUMMARY

Introduced later as controlled operations/accounting/inventory. Owner/Admin approval gated, audit-logged, financially safe.

## OTHER FUTURE MODULES SUMMARY

FMCG, Hospital/Clinic, School/Education, Property/Broker/Consultant — all future only, not implemented now.

## MODULE REGISTRY STRATEGY SUMMARY

Modules registered, enabled/disabled per tenant, permission controlled, UI/API/module-aware.

## IMPACT ON P3-005 AUTH/RBAC

- P3-005 must design RBAC with tenant, organization, module, role, permission support
- Roles not hardcoded only for marketplace
- Permissions support future modules
- Owner/Admin override controlled and audited

## IMPACT ON DATABASE/API DIRECTION

- Shared platform-core tables support all modules
- Business-module tables separate from platform-core
- API routes module-namespaced (`/api/v1/marketplace/*`, `/api/v1/crm/*`)
