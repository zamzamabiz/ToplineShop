- the ToplineShop-specific architecture and module boundary;
- an operator-first frontend proof sequence;
- hard dependencies on tenant, Auth/RBAC, audit, and module-control foundations;
- a measurable MVP vertical slice;
- bounded tasks from authorization through production readiness;
- data invariants, lifecycle states, quality rules, security controls, and operational evidence;
- branch, PR, verification, rollback, and WDR expectations;
- mandatory architecture decisions and stop conditions.
